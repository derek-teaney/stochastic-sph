\title{The equal-time statics at rest: entropy, momentum and shear stress.\\ Note and computational plan}
\author{D.~Teaney}
\affiliation{Department of Physics and Astronomy, Stony Brook University, Stony Brook, New York 11794, USA}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
Before the dynamics, the equilibrium of the model must be understood at equal times.  At fixed positions the particles are
independent, which fixes the structure of every equal-time correlation exactly: the momentum and the kinetic stress have no pair
correlations, and the entropy and the virial stress are correlated only through the positions.  What remains to be computed is the
single-particle distribution at fixed volume, exactly by quadrature, and the pair correlations of the volumes and of the anisotropy of the
stars in the Gaussian theory of the positions.  This note states the predictions and the measurements, and plans the hybrid Monte Carlo
campaign at NERSC that tests them.  The fluid is at rest; the bulk channel is left out.
\end{abstract}

\section{Goal and what is known}
\label{sec:goal}

The equal-time statics are the initial conditions of every correlator the dynamics will be compared with: the susceptibilities of the
hydrodynamic modes and, for the Helfand separation (\texttt{helfand.tex}), the equal-time pair correlation of the shear stresses of two
particles at separation $r$.  Hybrid Monte Carlo samples the equilibrium of the model exactly, so the statics can be settled without
the dynamics.

From \texttt{equilibrium.tex} (nine HMC runs, $L=9h$, $h/a=2,3,4$, $s_a=16,54,541$, i.e.\ $\sigma=p/(T\bar n)=4,13.5,135$):
\begin{itemize}
\item the positions follow the Gaussian theory at $\sigma\ge13.5$: $S(k)$, $g(r)$, the volume variance to $6\%$;
\item at $\sigma=4$ ($s_a=16$) four quantities miss by $14$--$23\%$ at every $h/a$: the volume variance and $S(k)$ (high), the entropy correlator
  $\avg{|S_{\bm k}|^2}/(L^2C_V)$ (low, at all $k$), and the momentum correlator $\avg{|M_{\bm k}|^2}/(L^2T(e+p))$ (high, flat in $k$, $1.14$--$1.17$).
\end{itemize}
The last two are single-particle variances.  The questions are whether the single-particle distribution at fixed volume accounts for all
four misses, and what the equal-time pair correlations of entropy, momentum and stress are.

\section{The structure: independence at fixed positions}
\label{sec:structure}

With the Liouville measure (the default) the equilibrium of the model at rest is
\st
\pi(\{\bm x,s,\bm m\})\propto\prod_a\exp\Big[s_a-\frac{e_a(s_a,\bm m_a,V_a)}{T}\Big],\qquad V_a=\frac1{n_a(\{\bm x\})},
\label{joint}
\stp
with $T$ the bare temperature of the bath.  At fixed positions it is a product over particles, each depending on its own volume only.  So,
exactly and at every $s_a$, with $\avg{\cdot|V}$ the average over one particle at fixed volume $V$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every single-particle moment is a function of $V_a$ alone, e.g.\ $\avg{s_a|V_a}$, $\avg{\delta s_a^2|V_a}$, $\avg{m_{a,i}m_{a,j}|V_a}$;
\item two different particles are correlated only through the positions:
  \st
  \avg{X_aY_b}=\Big\langle\avg{X_a|V_a}\,\avg{Y_b|V_b}\Big\rangle_{\{\bm x\}},\qquad a\neq b ;
  \stp
\item at rest the weight is even in each $\bm m_a$ and isotropic, so $\avg{\bm m_a|V_a}=0$ and the shear components of any tensor built from one
  particle's $(s_a,\bm m_a)$ have zero conditional mean.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Exact consequences.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Momentum}: $\avg{\bm m_a\cdot\bm m_b}=0$ for $a\neq b$, and $\avg{X_a\bm m_b}=0$ for any scalar $X$.  (Hybrid Monte Carlo redraws every momentum
  in its heat bath, so the total momentum is not fixed; in the dynamics it is, and $\avg{\bm m_a\cdot\bm m_b}=-\avg{\bm m^2}/(N-1)$.)
\item \emph{Entropy}: $\avg{\delta s_a\,\delta s_b}=\mathrm{Cov}\big(\avg{s|V_a},\avg{s|V_b}\big)$ for $a\neq b$: the pair correlation of the entropies is that of the volumes,
  mapped by $\avg{s|V}\simeq sV$.
\item \emph{Kinetic stress}: no pair correlation, and no correlation with the virial stress even for $a=b$ (\Sect{sec:stress}).
\end{itemize}
These are tests of the sampler and of the recorder, not of the theory.

\section{One particle at fixed volume}
\label{sec:single}

The single-particle distribution at volume $V$ is
\st
P(s,\bm m\,|\,V)\propto\exp\Big[s-\frac{e(s,\bm m,V)}{T}\Big],
\stp
in the variables the code uses, $(e,\bm m)$, with the target $S_a-\ln T_a$ (\texttt{vote\_entropy} with the Liouville flag).  It is computed by
quadrature on a grid (\texttt{particle\_moments} in \texttt{src/measure.jl} already does this for the mean and the covariance of $(e,\bm m)$).
Define the ratios to the Gaussian (continuum) values of \texttt{equilibrium.tex}, eq.~(A7) there,
\begin{keyeq}
\st
r_s(s_a)=\frac{\avg{\delta s^2|V}}{C_V\,V},\qquad r_m(s_a)=\frac{\avg{m_i^2|V}}{T(e+p)\,V},\qquad r_\tau(s_a)=\frac{\avg{(\tau^{\rm kin})^2|V}}{T^2},
\label{ratios}
\stp
\end{keyeq}
with $s_a=sV$ and $\tau^{\rm kin}$ the kinetic shear stress of \Sect{sec:stress}.  Each tends to $1$ as $s_a\to\infty$, with corrections of relative order
$1/s_a$ from the cubic and quartic terms about the maximum and from the relativistic energy (the light cone cuts off $|\bm v|<1$; at fixed
bare $T$ the momentum distribution is not Gaussian at $s_a=16$, where $\avg{\bm v^2}\simeq2/s_a\approx0.12$).  The same quadrature gives the
correction to $\ln Z_a(V)$, the coefficient $c$ of \texttt{equilibrium.tex}, eq.~(A8) there, which shifts the measure term of $S(k)$.

\paragraph{Beyond the saddle point: the weight of the positions.}  The exact weight of the positions is $\prod_aZ_a(V_a)$, of which \texttt{equilibrium.tex}
uses the saddle point $Z_a\propto V^{\alpha}e^{pV/T}$, with the phase-space exponent $\alpha=(d+1)/2$ (the entropy and the $d$ momentum components) and
the volume coefficient $\sigma=p/(T\bar n)$.  Keep $\ln Z_a(V)$ exact and fit it near $V=1/\bar n$ by the same form,
\st
\ln Z_a(V')\simeq\alpha_{\rm eff}\ln V'+\sigma_{\rm eff}\,\frac{V'}V+\text{const},\qquad
\alpha_{\rm eff}=-V^2\frac{d^2\ln Z_a}{dV^2},\qquad \sigma_{\rm eff}=V\frac{d\ln Z_a}{dV}+V^2\frac{d^2\ln Z_a}{dV^2} :
\label{alphasigma}
\stp
the effective phase-space exponent and volume coefficient, which tend to $\alpha$ and $\sigma$ as $s_a\to\infty$.  The expansion of \texttt{equilibrium.tex}, Appendix A, goes through unchanged with $V_a=V(1-\delta_a+\delta_a^2)$, and gives
\begin{keyeq}
\st
S(k)=\frac{1}{1+2\alpha_{\rm eff}\,\widetilde W\big(1-\tfrac12\widetilde W\big)+2\sigma_{\rm eff}\,\widetilde W\big(1-\widetilde W\big)},\qquad S(0)=\frac1{1+\alpha_{\rm eff}} ,
\label{Sexact}
\stp
\end{keyeq}
\texttt{equilibrium.tex}, eq.~(3) there, with $\alpha,\sigma\to\alpha_{\rm eff},\sigma_{\rm eff}$.  The labels are an ideal gas with $\mu_{\rm label}=(1+\alpha_{\rm eff})T\ln n$.  With the exact
$Z_a(V)$ the only approximation left in $S(k)$ is the second one, $\bar nh^2\gg1$.  $\alpha_{\rm eff}$ and $\sigma_{\rm eff}$ come from the same quadrature, at $s_a$ and its neighbours (a finite difference in $V$ at
fixed $T$), so Step 0 of \Sect{sec:plan} gives the improved $S(k)$ and volume variance at $\sigma=4$ with no new run.

\paragraph{Two integrals, two expansion parameters.}  The equilibrium has two layers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{The particle variables at fixed volume}, $Z_a(V)=\int ds\,d^2\bm m\,e^{s-e(s,\bm m,V)/T}$, are integrated exactly by quadrature: no saddle point.  The saddle point,
  $V^{(d+1)/2}e^{pV/T}$, has corrections of relative order $1/s_a$, and it is what failed at low $s_a$ (the momentum variance is $75\%$ above it at $s(T_0)V=10.5$).
\item \emph{The positions}, with the exact weight $\prod_aZ_a(V_a)$, are still treated in the Gaussian (random-phase) approximation: the weight is expanded to
  second order in $\delta_a=\delta n_a/\bar n$ about the uniform density, which needs $\ln Z_a$ only to second order, i.e.\ $\alpha_{\rm eff}$ and $\sigma_{\rm eff}$, and the modes $\delta\rho_{\bm k}$ are
  integrated as Gaussians.  The neglected terms, the third and higher derivatives of $\ln Z_a$ and the cubic terms in $\delta n$, are of relative order
  $\delta_a^2\sim1/(\bar nh^2)$.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
integral & expansion parameter & treatment \\
\midrule
$s_a$, $\bm m_a$ at fixed $V_a$ & $1/s_a$ & exact (quadrature) \\
positions & $1/(\bar nh^2)$ & Gaussian, with $\alpha_{\rm eff}$, $\sigma_{\rm eff}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
After Step 0 the residuals follow the second parameter: the volume variance is $8\%$ above the data at $h/a=2$ ($\bar nh^2=4.6$) and $1$--$2\%$ at $h/a=4$
($\bar nh^2=18.5$), and the core of $g(r)$ fails at $\sigma=135$, $h/a=2$.

\paragraph{Predictions to compare.}  If the single-particle distribution is the whole story,
\st
\frac{\avg{|M_{\bm k}|^2}}{L^2T(e+p)}=r_m\avg{V_a}\bar n,\qquad
\frac{\avg{|S_{\bm k}|^2}}{L^2C_V}=r_s+\frac{s^2}{\bar nC_V}(1-\widetilde W)^2S(k),
\stp
with no free parameter.  The HMC values at $s_a=16$ would require $r_m\approx1.15$ and $r_s\approx0.82$.  The Einstein measure differs by $\prod_aT_a$;
it changes $r_s,r_m$ at order $1/s_a$, and comparing the two separates the measure from the relativistic shape.

\section{Pair correlations}
\label{sec:pairs}

\paragraph{Definitions.}  For per-particle quantities $X_a$, $Y_a$, with $\delta X_a=X_a-\frac1N\sum_cX_c$ in each configuration,
\st
C_{XY}(r)=g(r)\,\avg{\delta X_a\,\delta Y_b}_r,\qquad \sum_{a\neq b}\delta X_a\delta Y_b=N\bar n\int d^2r\;C_{XY}(r),
\label{Cdef}
\stp
with $\avg{\cdot}_r$ the average over the pairs at separation $r$, as in \texttt{helfand.tex}: $C_{XY}$ is a pair-correlation function in the
sense of $g(r)$.  For a shear tensor with components $(\tau^{(1)},\tau^{(2)})=(\tau^{xy},\tfrac12(\tau^{xx}-\tau^{yy}))$ the bond angle $\phi_{ab}$ of
$\bm r_{ab}$ defines the projections
\st
\tau_\parallel=\tau^{(2)}\cos2\phi+\tau^{(1)}\sin2\phi,\qquad \tau_\perp=-\tau^{(2)}\sin2\phi+\tau^{(1)}\cos2\phi ,
\stp
and the isotropic pair functions are $C_{\tau\tau}=g\avg{\tau_a\cdot\tau_b}_r$ (with $\tau_a\cdot\tau_b=\tau_a^{(1)}\tau_b^{(1)}+\tau_a^{(2)}\tau_b^{(2)}$), its anisotropic
part $g\avg{\tau_{a\parallel}\tau_{b\parallel}-\tau_{a\perp}\tau_{b\perp}}_r$, and, for a scalar $X$ and a shear tensor, $C_{X\tau}=g\avg{\delta X_a\,\tau_{b\parallel}}_r$; the other
combinations vanish by isotropy and reflection.

\paragraph{Volumes and entropies.}  By \Sect{sec:structure},
\st
C_{ss}(r)\simeq s^2\,C_{VV}(r),\qquad C_{sV}(r)\simeq s\,C_{VV}(r),\qquad
\avg{\delta s_a^2}=r_s\,C_V\avg{V_a}+s^2\avg{\delta V_a^2} .
\stp
$C_{VV}(r)$ is a property of the positions.  In the Gaussian theory it has two parts: the indirect part, the density fluctuations seen by two
points at separation $r$,
\st
C_{VV}^{\rm ind}(r)\simeq g(r)\,\frac{V^2}{\bar n}\int\frac{d^2k}{(2\pi)^2}\,\widetilde W(k)^2S(k)\,J_0(kr),
\stp
and the direct part, each particle counting in the other's density, $\delta V_a\supset-V^2W(r)$.  The indirect part is suppressed on the kernel
scale by $S\sim1/\sigma$; the direct part is not, and their ratio is of order $\sigma/(\bar nh^2\ln\sigma)$, about $6$ at $\sigma=135$, $h/a=2$.  So the
fixed-point approximation used for the variance in \texttt{equilibrium.tex} is not enough for pair functions at large $\sigma$; the pair-conditioned
expansion (the two particles held, the rest Gaussian) is the derivation to do.

\paragraph{Momenta.}  $C_{m_im_j}(r)=0$ exactly in hybrid Monte Carlo.

\section{The shear stress and the Helfand moments at $t=0$}
\label{sec:stress}

\paragraph{The stress of a particle.}  From \texttt{helfand.tex}, eq.~(22) there, $V_aT_a^{ij}=v_a^im_a^j+P_a\,Q_a^{ij}$, with $P_a=p_aV_a^2$ and the
anisotropy of the star of $a$
\st
Q_a^{ij}=\sum_br_{ab}^i\,D_{ab}^j,\qquad Q_a\simeq n_a\one\ \text{(closure)} .
\stp
Its shear components are a kinetic and a virial part,
\st
\tau_a=\tau_a^{\rm kin}+\tau_a^{\rm vir},\qquad
\tau_a^{\rm kin}=\big(\tfrac12(v^xm^y+v^ym^x),\ \tfrac12(v^xm^x-v^ym^y)\big)_a,\qquad
\tau_a^{\rm vir}=P_a\,\mathring Q_a,
\stp
with $\mathring Q_a=(\tfrac12(Q^{xy}+Q^{yx}),\tfrac12(Q^{xx}-Q^{yy}))_a$ the shear part of the anisotropy, a function of the positions alone.

\paragraph{What is exact.}  $\tau^{\rm kin}_a$ depends on particle $a$'s own $(s_a,\bm m_a,V_a)$ and has zero conditional mean (\Sect{sec:structure}, item 3); $\tau^{\rm vir}_a$
has the conditional mean $\avg{P|V_a}\mathring Q_a$.  Therefore
\st
C_{\tau^{\rm kin}\tau^{\rm kin}}(r)=0,\qquad \avg{\tau_a^{\rm kin}\cdot\tau_b^{\rm vir}}=0\ \ (\text{all }a,b),\qquad
C_{\tau\tau}(r)=g(r)\Big\langle\avg{P|V_a}\avg{P|V_b}\,\mathring Q_a\cdot\mathring Q_b\Big\rangle_r .
\label{stressexact}
\stp
The pair correlation of the stresses is entirely positional, the correlation of the anisotropies of two stars, weighted by the pressures.
The self part has both: $\avg{\tau_a\cdot\tau_a}=\avg{\tau^{\rm kin}\cdot\tau^{\rm kin}}+\avg{(P\mathring Q)^2}$, with, in the Gaussian limit,
\st
\avg{(\tau_a^{(1)})^2}_{\rm kin}=\avg{(\tau_a^{(2)})^2}_{\rm kin}=T^2\,r_\tau .
\stp

\paragraph{The total: the infinite-frequency shear modulus.}  Summed over the box the stress is the momentum flux $J$ of \texttt{helfand.tex}, and the
equal-time sum rule
\st
\frac{\avg{(J^{(1)})^2}}{L^2T}=\frac1{L^2T}\Big[\sum_a\avg{(\tau_a^{(1)})^2}+N\bar n\int d^2r\,\tfrac12C_{\tau\tau}(r)\Big]\equiv G_\infty
\stp
defines the infinite-frequency shear modulus of the particles.  The kinetic part is $G_\infty^{\rm kin}=\bar nT\,r_\tau$: an ideal gas of labels.  The
virial part is the stiffness of the arrangement of the stars; it is the static side of the rigidity question (\texttt{rigidity.tex}), and in the
Gaussian theory it is a correlation of the anisotropy field $\mathring Q(\bm x)=\int d^2r\,K(\bm r)\,\rho(\bm x+\bm r)$, $K(\bm r)$ the shear part of $\bm r\,\bm D^T$, with a direct
and an indirect part as for the volumes.  To be derived; the measurement does not wait for it.

\paragraph{The Helfand moments.}  With the local attribution the Helfand displacement of a particle starts at zero, $g_a(0)=0$, so $C_g(r,0)=0$
identically; at short lags
\st
C_g(r,t)=t^2\,\avg{\tau_a\tau_b}_r+O(t^3)\quad(\text{one component}),
\stp
plus the stochastic increments, which are independent across particles at $t=0$.  The static input of the Helfand separation is therefore
$C_{\tau\tau}(r)$ of \Eq{stressexact}, with the self part $\avg{\tau_a^2}$ and the normalization $\avg{(\delta m^y)^2}$ of \texttt{helfand.tex}, $=r_mT(e+p)\avg{V_a}$.

\section{Computational plan}
\label{sec:plan}

\paragraph{Step 0 (laptop, minutes): the single-particle quadrature.}  A script, \texttt{examples/}\allowbreak\texttt{single\_particle.jl}: for $s_a=8$, $12$, $16$, $24$, $32$, $54$, $135$, $541$
and both measures, the ratios \Eq{ratios} and $\alpha_{\rm eff}$, $\sigma_{\rm eff}$ of \Eq{alphasigma}, from \texttt{particle\_moments} extended to the moments of $s$, $\bm m^2$,
$\bm m^4$ and $\tau^{\rm kin}$.  Compared at once with the nine existing runs ($r_m\approx1.15$, $r_s\approx0.82$ at $s_a=16$).  If the quadrature accounts
for the misses, the campaign below confirms it; if not, the campaign must find where they come from.  Run before the campaign.

\paragraph{Step 0 done (2026-11-17).}  The bath temperature of the runs is $T_0=0.870$, $0.960$, $0.996$ of $T(S_0)$ at the labels $s_a=16$, $54$, $541$, so
$p(T_0)/(T_0\bar n)=2.63$, $11.96$, $133.7$.  At $T_0$ the exact single particle gives $r_m=1.754$, $1.177$, $1.016$, $r_s=0.944$, $0.972$, $0.997$, $\alpha_{\rm eff}=1.15$, $1.40$, $1.49$,
$\sigma_{\rm eff}=2.79$, $12.00$, $133.7$, and
with them the momentum and entropy correlators, $S(k)$ and the volume variance of all nine runs agree with the predictions to $0$--$4\%$ ($8\%$ for the
volume variance at $h/a=2$).  Details: \texttt{equilibrium.tex}, Appendix B, ``The misses at $\sigma=4$''.  The Einstein set A$'$ is dropped: the measure changes
$r_m$, $\alpha_{\rm eff}$, $\sigma_{\rm eff}$ by less than $0.3\%$.  The campaign should set the bath temperature, not the start's entropy, as its parameter.

\paragraph{Code.}  \texttt{src/} changes, for the owner's approval; the dynamics and the sampler are untouched, and \texttt{test/run.sh all} must stay
bit-identical.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{StaticsRecorder} in \texttt{src/measure.jl}, called after each HMC update, recording per update:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{conditional moments} in 24 bins of $V_a\bar n$ ($0.7$--$1.3$): the count and the sums of $s_a$, $s_a^2$, $\bm m_a^2$, $\bm m_a^4$, $\ln T_a$,
    $(\tau_a^{\rm kin})^2$, $(\tau^{\rm vir}_a)^2$, $P_a$;

    $\avg{(\hat{\bm r}\cdot\bm m_a)(\hat{\bm r}\cdot\bm m_b)}_r$; for $\tau^{\rm kin}$ and $\tau^{\rm vir}$ separately and crossed, the isotropic and the $4\phi$ part; $C_{V\tau}$
    and $C_{s\tau}$ for $\tau^{\rm vir}$ (the $2\phi$ part).  Brute force over pairs with the minimum image, threaded ($\le2\times10^7$ pairs per update at $N=6000$);
  \item \emph{modes}: the existing $S_{\bm k}$, $M_{L,T}$, $N_{\bm k}$, plus $E_{\bm k}$, $V_{\bm k}=\sum_aV_ae^{-i\bm k\cdot\bm x_a}$ and the four stress modes (two components, kinetic
    and virial);
  \item \emph{totals}: $J^{(1)},J^{(2)}$, kinetic and virial, for $G_\infty$.
  \end{itemize}
\item The anisotropy $Q_a$ in the same loop as the smoothing (\texttt{kernel\_gradW}), on the cell list.
\item A guard in \texttt{hmc\_update!}: a trajectory whose equation-of-state inversion fails (\texttt{eos\_newton} to $e=-\infty$, \texttt{nersc/LOG.md}, 2026-11-16)
  is rejected instead of stopping the run.  Needed at $s_a\le8$; bit-identical where it does not fire.
\item \texttt{examples/statics.jl}: the driver, as \texttt{examples/volume\_fluctuations.jl} plus the recorder, honouring \texttt{SR\_SEED0} and \texttt{SR\_OUTDIR}
  (\texttt{nersc/array.sh}); arguments $L/h$, $h/a$, $s_a$, updates.
\item Tests (\texttt{test/teststatics.jl}): with $R_{\max}$ covering the box, the pair sums plus the self terms equal $(\sum_aX_a)^2$ to round-off; on the hexagonal
  lattice $\mathring Q_a=0$ and $\tr Q_a=2n_a$; with independent momenta drawn at fixed positions, $\avg{\bm m_a\cdot\bm m_b}_r$ is zero within its error.
\item Analysis, \texttt{examples/statics\_analysis.py}: the ratios against Step 0; the conditional moments against $V$; the pair functions against
  \Sect{sec:pairs}--\ref{sec:stress}; $G_\infty$ and its split.
\end{enumerate}
