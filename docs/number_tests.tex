\title{The deterministic tests with the number volume}
\author{D.~Teaney}
\affiliation{Department of Physics and Astronomy, Stony Brook University,
Stony Brook, New York 11794, USA}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
The number volume $V_a=1/n_a$ (\texttt{SPH\_VOLUME=number}, \texttt{volume\_n.tex}) changes the
energy-representation Hamiltonian wherever the entropy weights $s_a$ are unequal.  This note
runs the deterministic test suite in three configurations, \texttt{smoothed},
\texttt{:energy} with the entropy volume $V_a=s_a/S_a$ (the default), and \texttt{:energy}
with the number volume, and checks that nothing is broken.
\end{abstract}

%=============================================================================
\section{What was run}
\label{sec:run}

The suite of \texttt{energystep.tex} (``The deterministic test suite'') and the shear-wave
tests, each against an analytic solution:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{testgaussian}, \texttt{testgaussian\_1d}: a Gaussian bump of energy density
  in 2D and a stripe in 1D, small viscosity, to $t=2.2$.  The entropy weights are unequal from
  the start, so this is where the two volumes differ.
\item \texttt{testsound}: an ideal sound wave, one period.
\item \texttt{testsound\_viscous}, \texttt{testsound\_viscous\_1d}: a damped sound wave
  against $\cos(c_skt-\phi)\,e^{-\Gamma_sk^2t/2}$, one period.
\item \texttt{testdiffuse}: shear diffusion against $e^{-\nu k^2t}$.
\item \texttt{testshearwave}: the initial smoothing of the entropy and momentum densities.
\item \texttt{shearwave\_decay}: the shear wave with $\eta/s=0.02$, $0.002$ and $0$;
  \texttt{shearwave\_scan}: the ideal shear wave (transverse stiffness) for C2 and C6 at
  $h/a=2$, $3$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{How.}  One debug-queue node (job 61106930, 17 minutes), all runs side by side,
one thread each (\texttt{nersc/dtests.sh}).  The scripts plot as they go; in a batch job
\texttt{examples/plotsetup.jl} switches matplotlib to the Agg backend, which only writes
files, so they no longer crash on a compute node.  Figures are combined with
\texttt{examples/number\_tests\_montage.jl} on the login node.  All 24 runs exited cleanly.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Residuals are rms against the analytic solution; $\Delta H/H$ and $\Delta S/S$ at the end.
The \texttt{smoothed} and entropy-volume columns reproduce the table of \texttt{energystep.tex}
digit for digit.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\hline
test & & \texttt{smoothed} & entropy volume & number volume \\ \hline
Gaussian 2D, $t=2.2$ & $\Delta S/S$ & $0.0362$ & $0.0363$ & $0.0363$ \\
 & $\Delta H/H$ & $1.9\times10^{-5}$ & $2.4\times10^{-5}$ & $4.2\times10^{-5}$ \\
Gaussian 1D, $t=2.2$ & $\Delta S/S$ & $0.0512$ & $0.0513$ & $0.0514$ \\
 & $\Delta H/H$ & $1.4\times10^{-4}$ & $1.8\times10^{-4}$ & $1.7\times10^{-4}$ \\
viscous sound 2D & residual & $1.9\times10^{-4}$ & $3.4\times10^{-5}$ & $3.4\times10^{-5}$ \\
 & $\Delta H/H$ & $1.1\times10^{-8}$ & $4.0\times10^{-8}$ & $4.0\times10^{-8}$ \\
viscous sound 1D & residual & $2.2\times10^{-5}$ & $1.5\times10^{-5}$ & $1.5\times10^{-5}$ \\
 & $\Delta H/H$ & $7\times10^{-10}$ & $2.5\times10^{-9}$ & $2.5\times10^{-9}$ \\
ideal sound, one period & residual & $5.6\times10^{-3}$ & $1.8\times10^{-3}$ & $1.6\times10^{-3}$ \\
shear diffusion & residual & $1.55\times10^{-5}$ & $1.24\times10^{-5}$ & $1.24\times10^{-5}$ \\
shear wave, $\eta/s=0.02$ & $A(t_{\rm end})$ & $0.0809$ & $0.0797$ & $0.0797$ \\
 & $\Delta H/H$ & $7.7\times10^{-9}$ & $3.6\times10^{-8}$ & $3.6\times10^{-8}$ \\
ideal shear wave, C2, $h/a=2$ & $c_t$ & --- & $0.0340$ & $0.0340$ \\
ideal shear wave, C6, $h/a=2$ & $c_t$ & --- & $0.0170$ & $0.0170$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Uniform weights} (the sound waves, shear diffusion, the shear waves): the two
  volumes agree to the printed digits, as they must, since $V_a=1/n_a=s_a/S_a$ when all
  $s_a$ are equal; the viscous step makes the weights unequal only at the level $10^{-5}$.
  The ideal sound wave differs slightly ($1.6$ against $1.8\times10^{-3}$): its entropy
  weights carry the wave from the start.
\item \emph{Unequal weights} (the Gaussians): the entropy produced is the same to $0.1\%$, and
  the energy profiles are the same (\Fig{fig:gauss}).  The 2D energy drift is twice that of
  the entropy volume ($4.2$ against $2.4\times10^{-5}$), still small.  With the number volume the
  particle's energy density is its own, $s_an_a$ rather than the smoothed $S_a$, so the
  colour map of the particle energy densities reaches slightly higher values
  ($0.35$ against $0.33$); the smoothed slice is the same.
\item The transverse stiffness of the ideal lattice is unchanged ($c_t=0.0340$, C2, $h/a=2$):
  at uniform weights the number volume is the same function of the positions.
\end{enumerate}
Nothing is broken: the number volume passes every test as well as the entropy volume.
